01 / 06 · AI decision support

Decision Center

One business question. A structured management report backed by traceable evidence.

Workflow at a glance

Business question→Verified evidence sources→Decision-support report

Why it matters

Business evidence is spread across documents, email and ERP data. Decision-makers need a coherent view without losing the source of each claim.

The solution

An AI decision-support system designed to assemble approved business evidence into structured executive reports, surface conflicting information and retain human review before release.

What it covers

  • Source-linked findings
  • Conflicting evidence made visible
  • Human review of reports

My involvement

Developing AI workflows and the integration of business evidence into decision-support reporting.

The available project documentation describes phased development. This page does not claim live production deployment.
