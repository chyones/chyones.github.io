A CLOSER LOOK AT THE PRODUCT

What Decision Center actually includes

An evidence-first executive reporting system, not a generic question-answer chatbot.

01

Cross-system evidence

Built to bring authorized information from enterprise documents, email, and ERP records into one analysis workflow.

02

Evidence-linked reporting

Structures findings alongside their sources, so decision-makers can trace what supports each conclusion.

03

Conflict and gap handling

Surfaces conflicting records and missing evidence instead of treating every source as equally reliable.

04

Controlled AI pipeline

Uses retrieval, normalization, analysis, deterministic claim checks and report quality gates.

05

Human approval workflow

Separates generated reports from reviewed and approved material before publication.

06

Report workspace

Source includes report composition, processing, evidence viewing, exports and approval-related screens.

07

Administration and audit

Backend-connected administration screens cover connectors, access, source mapping, system health and audit visibility.

08

Repeatable validation

Project records document a 64-case evaluation set and 54/54 browser acceptance tests at the referenced phase.

Why it matters

Business evidence is spread across documents, email and ERP data. Decision-makers need a coherent view without losing the source of each claim.

The solution

An AI decision-support system designed to assemble approved business evidence into structured executive reports, surface conflicting information and retain human review before release.

What it covers

  • Source-linked findings
  • Conflicting evidence made visible
  • Human review of reports

My involvement

Developing AI workflows and the integration of business evidence into decision-support reporting.
